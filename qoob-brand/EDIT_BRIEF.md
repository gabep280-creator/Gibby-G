## Target style: native TikTok / Reels / Shorts creator-review ad
Feels like a creator post, not a corporate spot. Retention-first.
1. Pacing: tight jump cuts. Remove dead air between phrases (leave ~0.08s breath).
   Do NOT cut the "wait for it..." pause before the notification — that's the suspense beat.
2. Punch-ins: alternate 100% / 112% framing on each jump cut so cuts feel deliberate,
   framing anchored on the face. No zoom on the screen-recording insert.
3. Captions: bold, centered, 1–3 words at a time, word-synced, Inter Display Black,
   white with heavy shadow; key words highlighted in the Qoob brand gradient
   (purple -> blue -> teal). Quick pop-in scale. Kept in the safe zone (chest height),
   moved up during the screen-recording insert so they don't cover the app UI/PiP.
   Spell the brand "Qoob" (audio says "Cube").
4. Brand: open with a Qoob Search app-icon lockup pill (first ~2s, while he says the name),
   small "01 · ALERTS" chapter tag when the feature starts, and a 2.8s branded end card
   (logo animation + "Qoob Search" + tagline lifted from his own verdict line).
5. Grade: light contrast/saturation lift, soft vignette — keep skin natural.
6. Audio: keep his voice clean, de-click every cut with micro-fades, loudness to -14 LUFS
   (platform standard). Subtle pop on the intro lockup, soft whoosh into the end card.
   No music baked in — platform-trending sounds get added at upload.
7. Safe zones: nothing important in the bottom ~18% or right ~12% (platform UI).

Rebuild the icon as clean vector: four rings in a diamond around a diagonal stem
(blue "d"-bowl + teal "p"-bowl on the stem, purple and cyan teardrops behind),
